## App Introduction

### English

**Family Word Party**  
**Subtitle:** Guess words with family

Family Word Party is a fun word guessing game for families, friends, classrooms, and parties. One player gives clues with hints, gestures, or simple descriptions, while others try to guess the word without hearing the answer itself. The app includes multiple language options, level-based word banks, Practice Mode, and Timed Challenge Mode for fast and lively rounds.
